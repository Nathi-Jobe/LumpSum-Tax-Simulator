Why is it not a flat percentage?

Lump sums are taxed in bands. The first part is tax-free, and each higher part is taxed at a higher rate (18%, then 27%, then 36%). A bigger lump sum therefore has a higher overall percentage.

What are the three pots in a two-pot fund?
  • Vested component: generally accumulated before 1 September 2024, plus growth. Its cash-at-retirement rights depend partly on the fund type and any pre-2021 protected provident benefits.
  • Savings component: generally one-third of new contributions since 1 September 2024, subject to fund allocations and initial seeding (generally 10% of the 31 August 2024 interest, capped at R30,000, where applicable). Pre-retirement withdrawals usually require at least R2,000 and are taxed as ordinary income; special exit exceptions can apply. At retirement, any remaining savings-component balance may be taken as part of a retirement lump sum and taxed under the retirement lump-sum table, not the pre-retirement ordinary-income rules.
  • Retirement component: generally two-thirds of new contributions. Ordinarily preserved to retirement for an annuity, subject to exceptions including the small-benefit rule.
Why does the simulator ask about earlier lump sums?

SARS counts your lump sums over your whole lifetime, not one at a time. Earlier payouts may have used lower tax bands, so a later benefit can be taxed at higher rates. For example, R400,000 gross from an earlier resignation and R200,000 gross from a later retirement payout means you have R600,000 of prior relevant gross benefits to report (subject to the applicable SARS dates and classifications).

Retiring or leaving your job? Know your options

Your fund and your severance pay are two different things. Decide on each separately.

At retirement

Cash plus annuity

You can usually take the savings component, one-third of non-protected vested benefits, and any confirmed protected provident rights in cash. Small-benefit exceptions and fund rules may change the result.

Leaving your job

Take cash now

Depending on your fund and exit rules, vested-component cash generally uses the withdrawal lump-sum table, while any savings-component withdrawal uses normal income tax. The retirement component is generally preserved.

Leaving your job

Preserve your fund

An eligible tax-neutral transfer can preserve retirement savings. Retirement lump-sum tax is calculated under a different table; the first R550,000 may be tax-free only if prior relevant lump sums have not used it up.

If you receive a qualifying employer severance benefit, it is separate from your fund and may be taxed using the retirement/severance table. Do not apply this tax treatment to ordinary resignation, leave or notice pay. Leave pay, notice pay and bonuses are taxed as normal salary and are not included.

Your tax-free amount

The tax tables apply cumulatively to qualifying lump sums. Earlier benefits may affect your remaining lower-tax bands.

R550,000 for lump sums taken at retirement, and for severance packages, counted over your whole lifetime.

R27,500 is the first tax-free band on the withdrawal table, subject to cumulative prior qualifying benefits.

Savings-component withdrawals before retirement are taxable as ordinary income, with tax dependent on your full tax position. At retirement, the remaining savings-component cash is included in the retirement lump sum and normally falls under the retirement/severance lump-sum tax table, subject to SARS history and applicable deductions.

Cashing out when you leave your job can use up part of the R550,000 you may prefer to keep for retirement. Speak to an adviser before you cash out.

Method

SARS tables for the 2026/27 tax year (1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027). The lump sum tables have not changed since 1 March 2023.

What each choice does in the simulator
  • Retire: maximum cash is savings plus protected provident rights and one-third of the remaining vested component. From 1 March 2026, the general R360,000 de minimis threshold corresponds, under the two-pot calculation, to an ordinarily annuitisable amount of R240,000 or less (retirement component plus two-thirds of non-protected vested value). The total fund balance alone is not a sufficient test for eligibility to take everything in cash. Actual protected rights must be verified with your fund.
  • Leave and take cash: models an occupational pension/provident withdrawal from the vested component and only includes savings-component withdrawal if elected and eligibility confirmed. This is not an automatic entitlement. A qualifying severance payment is modelled first if independently verified.
  • Leave and preserve: no tax today. An eligible preservation or tax-neutral transfer may defer tax; this illustration uses today's balances, excludes future investment returns, fees and rule changes, and does not imply that a preservation fund offers no permitted pre-retirement withdrawal.
Retirement and severance table

  • R770,001 to R1,155,000: R39,600 plus 27% above R770,000

Savings pot withdrawals (income tax table)

Before-retirement savings withdrawals are taxed as extra ordinary income. This indicative calculation compares ordinary income tax with and without the pre-retirement withdrawal using 2026/27 rates and age-group rebates when supplied. SARS directives, age rebates, medical credits, deductions, other income and outstanding tax can change actual proceeds.

How the lump sum tax is worked out

Qualifying earlier GROSS benefits are added to the new lump sum and the applicable current benefit table is applied to both totals. The difference is an indicative marginal benefit tax, before tax-directive deductions and adjustments. Exact SARS treatment depends on the benefit type, gross history, date and fund records.

What this simulator does not cover
  • Leave pay, notice pay and bonuses (taxed as salary).
  • The tax on monthly annuity income.
  • Fund-specific rules and exceptions; tax-directive deductions, historical classifications and SARS adjustments; medical credits, other income and allowable personal deductions not included in the supplied taxable-income figure.
  • Previously disallowed/non-deductible retirement fund contributions may reduce the taxable retirement lump sum under applicable tax rules. This simulator does not calculate that adjustment; consult SARS or the fund for a formal directive.
  • Whether a particular severance qualifies, preservation transfer rules, and prior two-pot withdrawal eligibility.
  • GEPF, unknown fund types, unsupported RA and preservation-fund employment-exit scenarios: this app intentionally withholds a numeric estimate.
